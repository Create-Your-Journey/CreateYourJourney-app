Install on iPhone

Open this app in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen. Your trips and receipts are stored in this browser on this device.

Sync with iCloud Drive

Save a sync file to iCloud Drive, then open this app on your other device and import the same file. This carries your trips, bookings, customers, expenses and receipt photos.

This is a manual sync: save the newest encrypted file to iCloud Drive, then unlock the app with the same passphrase on your other device and import it. This is manual, not live sync; deletions and edits to existing records do not merge. Use one device at a time.

Protecting your records
